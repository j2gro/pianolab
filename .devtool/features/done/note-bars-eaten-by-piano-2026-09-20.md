# Note bars eaten by piano

When falling note bars reach the keyboard hit line, clip them so they are consumed by the keys instead of drawing over the piano. Add a contact glow at the clip edge, matching Synthesia-style visualizers.

Verified in Listen mode: bars shorten at the gold hit line and no longer draw over the keys.
